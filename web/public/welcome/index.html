How it works

Three taps to a conversation.

  1. 1 Type it

    A big box, big text. Enter speaks it. Typos are fixed on the way out, while your names and ALL-CAPS words stay as typed.

  2. 2 Hear it

    Your phone speaks in an ElevenLabs voice you pick, a free Microsoft voice, or the phone's own. Tap anything you said to say it again.

  3. 3 Listen, then tap a reply

    Listen transcribes the other person. Four short replies appear, written for what they just said. Tap one and it is spoken, or edit it first.

Built for a real week without a voice

Small, fast, and yours.

  • No account, no databaseSettings and phrases live on your phone. Your ElevenLabs key is yours, sent with each request and never stored on the server.
  • Your voice in the meetingA virtual cable carries the voice into Teams or Zoom as your microphone, and Speechless listens to the call instead of the room. The screen is never recorded, only speech is transcribed. Setup guide
  • Fast enough to keep upReplies are proposed by a small, quick model. If anything is slow or down, the phone's own voice steps in so you are never stuck silent.
  • Open source, self-hostableA small FastAPI server and a PWA. Run it on your own box with your own keys, or use the hosted one.
